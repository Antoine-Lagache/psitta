Adaptations by Antoine Lagache / Psitta
The source material has been selected, filtered, ordered and grouped into
1,297 word exercises and 1,357 sentence groups (3,452 Japanese-English instances).
Selected dictionary senses have been reformatted, and separate question/answer
HTML fragments with furigana have been generated. On 15 flagged sentence cards,
the Tatoeba translation is preserved alongside an explanation and an AI-generated
suggestion explicitly marked as not independently verified.
