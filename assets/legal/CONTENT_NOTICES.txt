OpenJLPT and its upstream contributors
OpenJLPT vocabulary lists were used to select the N5/N4 vocabulary. Its grammar
files are also recorded among the editorial source downloads. The frozen
displayed meanings come from JMdict and displayed sentences from Tatoeba.
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT
