Tatoeba — Japanese and English sentences

The sentence exercises in Psitta contain textual sentences from Tatoeba and
its contributors: https://tatoeba.org/

The sentences are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

Psitta selects direct Japanese-English translation pairs, groups and orders
them for practice, and renders them as question/answer HTML. No Tatoeba audio
recordings are included in this corpus. The source export dates are recorded
in the Psitta learning-content notice.

Tatoeba's published attribution guidance:
https://en.wiki.tatoeba.org/articles/show/faq

The original sentence IDs and contributor metadata are retained in the
psitta-content editorial reference package for source traceability.
Tatoeba and its contributors do not endorse Psitta. The data is provided
without warranty; the complete license follows.
