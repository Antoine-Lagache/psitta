The approach

Meet words. Practise patterns. Revisit.

01 / VOCABULARY

Start with a word

Study Japanese words with their readings and English meanings. Furigana helps you read the kanji as you go.

02 / SENTENCES

See the structure again

Practise related sentences within a group. Each group shares a review schedule, while individual examples track their own exposure.

03 / REPETITION

Build a routine

Grade your answers, review when exercises become due, and resume unfinished sessions. Follow your activity with daily and overall statistics.

Built in the open

A learning project,
in more ways than one.

Psitta is developed by Antoine Lagache. Built with Flutter, it explores language learning alongside the design of a modular study engine and a documented spaced repetition model.

Explore the source code →

Content with provenance

Vocabulary draws on JMdict; sentences and their English translations come from Tatoeba. Source attribution is available in the app. Editorial choices and known limitations are documented.

Psitta is a vocabulary and sentence practice tool, intended to accompany other learning resources. The ordering and scheduling are design choices, not a claim of measured learning effectiveness.
